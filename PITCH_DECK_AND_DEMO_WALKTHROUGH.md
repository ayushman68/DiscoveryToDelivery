## 📽️ 6-Slide Pitch Deck

### Slide 1: The Problem — Traditional Marketplaces Fail AI-Native Workflows
* **Market Friction**: Upwork and Fiverr treat creators like Photoshop freelancers. They do not capture diffusion checkpoints, LoRAs, samplers, or generation seeds.
* **Brand Fear**: 78% of enterprise brands hesitate to commission generative creators due to fear of copyright infringement, dirty training data, and inability to reproduce or adjust assets.
* **The Gap**: Brands need verified AI toolstacks, audited prompt provenance, and milestone-locked escrow tied to generation iterations.

### Slide 2: The Solution — DiscoveryToDelivery
* **AI-Native Marketplace Engine**: Purpose-built for AI filmmakers, animators, and generative stylists.
* **End-to-End Protocol**: Connects brands from discovery through tool-verified portfolios, AI-assisted brief creation, iterative generation passes, and cryptographic commercial rights transfer.
* **Venture Studio Thesis**: Applied AI vertical infrastructure designed to scale commercial generative production for Kampus.VC's venture portfolio.

### Slide 3: Creator Profiles & Granular AI Portfolios (30% Evaluation Weight)
* **Beyond JPEGs & MP4s**: Every portfolio item inspects the exact generative pipeline:
  * Primary Models: Flux.1 Pro, Runway Gen-3 Alpha, Kling 1.5, ComfyUI, ElevenLabs
  * Positive & Negative Prompts with one-click copy
  * Sampler (Euler Ancestral), CFG Scale (6.5), Seed (`8941042781`), and Upscale Pipeline
* **One-Click ComfyUI Export**: Brands can download the exact deterministic node graph (`.json`) to verify visual reproducibility.

### Slide 4: Discovery-to-Delivery Lifecycle Engine (Core Scope)
* **Stage 1 (Pitch)**: Creator submits pitch with sample styleframe seeds and proposed toolstack.
* **Stage 2 (Escrow Lock)**: Brand accepts proposal; milestone funds are locked in escrow and commercial terms sealed.
* **Stage 3 (Generation Passes)**: Multi-pass iteration loop where creators upload intermediate passes and brands leave prompt adjustment feedback.
* **Stage 4 (Deliverables Audit)**: 4K Master ProRes, ComfyUI node graph, and seed audit report submitted.
* **Stage 5 (Commercial Certification)**: Escrow released; SHA-256 tamper-proof certificate minted.

### Slide 5: Bonus Innovations (+10% Bonus Credit)
* **AI-Assisted Brief Builder (+5%)**: Turns a rough 1-sentence prompt into a complete structured brief with aspect ratios, suggested models, styleframe prompts, and milestone breakdown (powered by Groq Llama-3.3-70B API with neural fallback).
* **Creator Verification Signals & Cryptographic Certificates (+5%)**: Trust badges for Verified Tools, Model Indemnity Clearance, and SHA-256 tamper-proof digital certificates.

### Slide 6: Business Model & Studio Scalability
* **Take Rate**: 8% platform fee on milestone escrow releases + 4% commercial indemnity clearance fee.
* **Enterprise Studio SaaS**: Private creator clusters and bespoke workflow auditing for agencies.
* **Venture Moat**: Proprietary database of commercial diffusion workflows, verified creator trust scores, and automated legal indemnity protocol.
